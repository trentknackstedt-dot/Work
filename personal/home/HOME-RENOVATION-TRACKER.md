## 🏠 Basement Decluttering Strategy

### **Phase 1: Inventory & Sort (Weeks 1-2)**

**Step 1: Complete Inventory**
- [ ] Take photos of everything in basement
- [ ] Create item list (use GitHub file)
- [ ] Note condition of each item
- [ ] Identify what actually gets used

**Step 2: The 4-Box Method**
- [ ] Box 1: KEEP (regularly used, valuable, sentimental)
- [ ] Box 2: SELL (good condition, worth money)
- [ ] Box 3: DONATE (good condition, no value)
- [ ] Box 4: TRASH (broken, unusable)

**Step 3: Decision Making**
- **Ask for each item:** "Have I used this in 12 months?"
- **If NO:** Consider selling/donating
- **If YES but rarely:** Can you access it easily?
- **Be ruthless:** 50% reduction target

### **Phase 2: Removal & Cleanup (Weeks 2-3)**

- [ ] Schedule donation pickup (Goodwill, Salvation Army)
- [ ] List items for sale (Facebook Marketplace, Craigslist)
- [ ] Haul/trash remaining items
- [ ] Deep clean empty spaces
- [ ] Clean/paint walls if needed

### **Phase 3: Organization System (Week 3-4)**

- [ ] Install shelving units (heavy-duty metal preferred)
- [ ] Label everything clearly
- [ ] Create zones: Tools, Sports, Holiday, Storage, Projects
- [ ] Keep pathways clear
- [ ] Document final layout with photos

### **What to Keep vs. Declutter**

**KEEP:**
- ✅ Tools actively used (home, car, hobbies)
- ✅ Hobby equipment (brewing, cannabis, car, gym)
- ✅ Seasonal items (holiday decorations)
- ✅ Important documents (boxed, labeled)
- ✅ Sentimental items (limited quantity)

**SELL:**
- ✅ Furniture in good condition
- ✅ Sports equipment not used
- ✅ Electronics (outdated but working)
- ✅ Books (unless reference)
- ✅ Duplicate items

**DONATE:**
- ✅ Clothing you don't wear
- ✅ Kitchen items (duplicates)
- ✅ Books you won't re-read
- ✅ Furniture no longer needed
- ✅ Anything usable but unwanted

**TRASH:**
- ✅ Broken items (unfixable)
- ✅ Old paint/chemicals (proper disposal)
- ✅ Damaged furniture
- ✅ Expired items
- ✅ Single socks, broken tools

### **Color Selection Tips**

**For Resale Value:**
- ✅ Neutral base colors (white, gray, beige)
- ✅ Avoid bold colors (limit to accent walls)
- ✅ Matte finish walls (professional look)
- ✅ Glossy/semi-gloss on trim (traditional, classy)

**Recommended Palette:**
- Walls: Warm white or light gray
- Trim: Crisp white or soft cream
- Accent walls: Soft blue or sage (optional)
